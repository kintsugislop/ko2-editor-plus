Project guide

Scenes (made with shift+main) are laid end to end; each lasts as long as its longest pattern and shorter ones loop inside it. SONG plays the project's song-mode list instead: any scenes in any order, repeats allowed, up to 99 steps — edit it here (+ add scene, ◀ ▶ to move, ⧉ ✕) and send it like any other change. Click the ruler to jump. Playback and stems run here, from your own samples — pad pitch, trim and volume applied, and the fader automation for LEVEL, PAN, PITCH, TUNE, LPF and HPF (lanes marked ▶). VEL and MOD fader moves are included as MIDI controllers.
